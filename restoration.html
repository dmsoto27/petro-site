Insurance Restoration FAQ

Common questions from homeowners after water, fire or storm damage.

Do I have to use my insurance company’s preferred contractor?

No. Your insurance company may suggest contractors from its preferred list, but in California you have the right to choose your own contractor for repairs. You can ask your adjuster any questions about how your policy handles that choice.

What should I do first after water damage?

Make sure everyone is safe, stop the water at the source if you can (shut off the main water valve), and turn off power to wet areas only if it is safe to do so. Take photos and video of the damage before anything is moved or thrown away, then contact your insurance company to start a claim and call us at (661) 554-9217 to schedule an inspection and estimate.

Do you work with my insurance company?

We work with homeowners who are filing claims with their insurance company. We document the damage, provide a detailed written estimate, and communicate with your adjuster so the scope of repairs is clear. Your coverage and payment are determined by your policy and your insurer.
