Pre-Approved ADU Plans & Local Programs

Cities and the County offer ready-made ADU plans that can save design time. We can build from these plans or from a custom design.

City of Lancaster

Pre-approved ADU plans designed by RRM Design Group, sold through the City’s permit portal. Five designs, including:

  • Poppy: 1 bed / 1 bath, 350 sq ft
  • Juniper: 1 bed / 1 bath, 602 sq ft
  • Goldenbrush: 2 bed / 2 bath, 799 sq ft
  • Primrose: 2 bed / 2 bath, 840 sq ft
  • Sunflower

A project-specific site plan is still required.

Lancaster ADU Assistance Loan

The City offers a deferred-payment, no-interest loan of up to $10,000 for ADU soft costs. It may be forgiven over five years if the program conditions are met, including owner-occupancy and renting the ADU to a low-income household. The application asks for a preliminary ADU cost estimate signed by a licensed contractor, which we can provide.

Lancaster ADU Assistance Loan →

City of Palmdale

Free standard ADU concept plans in Craftsman and Spanish styles:

  • Plan 1: 608 sq ft
  • Plan 2: 749 sq ft
  • Plan 3: about 1,000–1,106 sq ft

These are conceptual plans; construction drawings, structural calculations and Title 24 energy calculations are still needed. We handle that.

LA County (unincorporated areas)

For unincorporated communities such as Acton, Lake Los Angeles, Littlerock, Quartz Hill, Stevenson Ranch and Castaic, LA County Public Works offers free pre-approved ADU plans:

  • Plan A: 1,200 sq ft, 3 bed / 1 bath
  • Plan B: 1,200 sq ft, 2 bed / 1 bath
  • Plan C: 800 sq ft, 1 bed / 1 bath

A site-specific plot plan is still required.

LA County pre-approved ADU plans →

City of Santa Clarita

Santa Clarita has its own ADU rules. Per the City’s ADU handout, a new detached ADU can be up to 800 sq ft with 4-foot side and rear setbacks and a 16-foot height limit, junior ADUs can be up to 500 sq ft, and no parking is required.

Typical ADU Timeline

An ADU happens in two phases: design and permits, then construction.

1. Design & plans

Pre-approved or standard plans can shorten this phase. Custom designs take longer.

2. Permits

Plan review times vary by city. For example, the City of Lancaster says a first plan review usually takes 2–3 weeks and each re-review 1–2 weeks.

3. Construction

Depends on the size and type of ADU. Garage conversions are usually faster than new detached builds. You get a schedule with your written estimate.

ADU FAQ

Common questions from homeowners thinking about an ADU.

Do I need a permit for an ADU or garage conversion?

Yes. A legal ADU, including a garage conversion, needs a building permit. We handle the plans and permits for you. In the City of Lancaster, ADUs do not need Planning approval and go straight to permitting.

How big can my ADU be?

It depends on your lot and where you live. For example, Santa Clarita allows new detached ADUs up to 800 sq ft, Lancaster’s pre-approved plans range from 350 to 840 sq ft, and LA County’s pre-approved plans for unincorporated areas are 800 and 1,200 sq ft. Junior ADUs are limited to 500 sq ft. We’ll confirm what fits on your property.

Can I rent out my ADU?

In most cases, yes. For example, Santa Clarita allows an ADU to be rented separately from the main home (not for less than 30 days), but not sold separately. If you use Lancaster’s ADU Assistance Loan, the program requires renting the ADU to a low-income household. Rules vary by city, so we’ll go over yours.

What about utilities, sewer or septic?

Water, sewer or septic, electrical and gas connections are part of every ADU plan. During the site visit we look at your existing utilities, your electrical panel and, on septic properties, what the system and the agencies will require. These items can affect cost, so we include them in your estimate.

Are there city impact fees or school fees?

City impact fees are waived for ADUs under 750 sq ft; larger ADUs pay a reduced, proportional amount. School fees don’t apply to ADUs under 500 sq ft. Permit, plan check and utility connection fees still apply. We’ll include expected fees in your estimate.
